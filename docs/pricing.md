# Pricing — Steiner Budget Pro (canonical, owner-ruled 2026-11-08)

Supersedes the $7.99–$12.99 guidance band of feasibility §3.4 (decision 9)
with a firm ruling. Structure unchanged from decision 12: **separate paid
Play listing** (`pro` flavor, `.pro` suffix, data-preserving handoff), coffee
tips the only IAP, nothing gated inside the free tier, no ads, ever.

## Ruling

| Moment | Price (US storefront) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **v1.0 launch (M2, ~Dec 2026)** | **$9.99 one-time** | Regional pricing on; standard refund policy |
| **iOS activation (M4)** | **$11.99–$12.99** | iOS cohort pays more (Bluecoins+ $15.99 iOS precedent); Android listing rises with it |
| Always | Existing Pro owners **grandfathered for life** | Never-charged-upgrade promise; pairs with the "more, never less" rule |

## Measured basis (2026-11-08 analysis)
- Niche comp: Bluecoins+ one-time $15.99 iOS / ~$7–10 Android — app lacks our
  on-device OCR and has no own-cloud automation; Money Manager premium ~$5–7
  (their-cloud). Subscription incumbents ($109–179/yr) are the anchor we sit
  92% below.
- Flagship value = own-cloud scheduled E2EE backup (unique at any price in the
  offline-first niche) + scan queue + widgets + analytics pack = three felt
  levers (safety / time / depth).
- Payback math at $9.99 (net $8.49 after Play 15%): ~247 copies clears the
  $2,100 trademark spend (Jan-2025 USPTO schedule, 3 marks × 2 classes); health
  gate ≥0.5% unlock conv at ≥8k installs.

## Experiment surface
Play Console price tests are available at zero code cost — the ruling sets the
*default*, not a prohibition on A/B (e.g., $7.99 vs $9.99 vs $12.99 once
install volume supports significance). Structural guardrails that never move:
free tier stays feature-complete; nothing once-free becomes paid; no ads;
no subscription (decision 12 amendment would require an owner ruling).
