Supersedes the $7.99–$12.99 guidance band of feasibility §3.4 (decision 9)
with a firm ruling. Structure unchanged from decision 12: **separate paid
Play listing** (`pro` flavor, `.pro` suffix, data-preserving handoff), coffee
tips the only IAP, nothing gated inside the free tier, no ads, ever.

## Basis of the 2026-11-17 re-ruling ($9.99 → $5.99)

- Portfolio strategy: Budget is the trust proof for the Steiner line (Vault
  follows spring 2027). The cheapest honest question — "is this studio
  for real?" — is answered by the price, not the pledge page.
- Anchor story strengthens: $5.99 one-time sits ~95% under the $109–179/yr
  subscription incumbents ($5.99 ÷ $109 ≈ 5.5% of an annual sub).
- Payback math at $5.99 (net $5.09 after Play 15%): $2,100 ÷ $5.09 ≈ **413
  copies** clears the trademark spend (Jan-2025 USPTO schedule, 3 marks × 2
  classes) — revenue recovery is slower by design; the ruling accepts that.
- Support-cost watch: the own-WebDAV backup is the highest-support feature in
  the pack; a lower price widens who attempts the setup. The countermeasure
  is onboarding quality, not price.

## Measured basis (2026-11-08 analysis, historical)
- Niche comp: Bluecoins+ one-time $15.99 iOS / ~$7–10 Android — app lacks our
  on-device OCR and has no own-cloud automation; Money Manager premium ~$5–7
  (their-cloud). Subscription incumbents ($109–179/yr) are the anchor we sit
  92% below (at the then-$9.99 ruling; ~95% below at $5.99).
- Flagship value = own-cloud scheduled E2EE backup (unique at any price in the
  offline-first niche) + scan queue + widgets + analytics pack = three felt
  levers (safety / time / depth).

## Experiment surface
Play Console price tests remain available at zero code cost — the ruling sets
the *default*, not a prohibition on A/B once install volume supports
significance. Structural guardrails that never move: free tier stays
feature-complete; nothing once-free becomes paid; no ads; no subscription
(decision 12 amendment would require an owner ruling).
