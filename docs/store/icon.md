## Batch 2 concepts (glyph names the job — no label needed)

| concept | the job it signals | mass-market read | confusion risk | 32 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **jar** | *the app itself*: fill toward the limit line; coin dropping in; limit tick = the green-until-red rule. Marks flank the wall at fill level (steel Free / amber Pro — owner caught the first draft's paper-on-paper free-tier line) | personal finance / savings goal | water-tracker (killed by tick+coin) | ✅ tick+fill+coin all survive |
| **piggy** | the universal budgeting animal; coin into slot | instant "budget/savings" | kids' savings apps, banking | ✅ blob+coin readable |
| **envelope** | envelope budgeting (the YNAB-school term of art) | budgeting / expense | **mail/email apps** — real | ✅ |
| **dial** | progress vs target (the Reports gauge) | analytics / habit tracker | speedometer/fitness | ⚠️ needle+arc mush — self-eliminated |

Advisory vision pass (ollamatest, verified against pixels): best =
piggy, runner-up = jar; envelope correctly read as budgeting but with the
mail caveat; dial flagged weak at 32 px — matches my own read.

## Agent recommendation (owner asked for opinions)

**jar** as primary pick: it is the only glyph that depicts what the app
actually *does* — a container filling toward an honest limit — and the
amber limit-tick is the brand's "green until red" promise in one stroke.
**piggy** is the strongest mass-market alternative (advisor's pick): loudest
category signal, weakest personality, and "cute savings" shade risks
cheapening a household-records tool. If the beta tester instantly says
"budget app" either way, jar wins on uniqueness + honesty of metaphor.

Batch 1 (wallet/receipt family, `docs/store/icon-candidates/`) retained for
the record; owner ruled it too generic to convey the job.
