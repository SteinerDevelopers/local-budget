## Agent recommendation (owner asked for opinions)

**jar** as primary pick: it is the only glyph that depicts what the app
actually *does* — a container filling toward an honest limit — and the
amber limit-tick is the brand's "green until red" promise in one stroke.
**piggy** is the strongest mass-market alternative (advisor's pick): loudest
category signal, weakest personality, and "cute savings" shade risks
cheapening a household-records tool. If the beta tester instantly says
"budget app" either way, jar wins on uniqueness + honesty of metaphor.

Batch 1 (wallet/receipt family, `docs/store/icon-candidates/`) retained for
the record; owner ruled it too generic to convey the job.

## Batch 4 — the reasoning walk (2026-11-06 late night)

Owner rejected jar-execution ("more like an ink bottle") and asked for a
thought-through hunt for *private budget, in Vault's theme*, several
iterations allowed. Batches 3+4 explored: coin-stack (→ drum), ledger book
(→ guidebook), envelope-mail, billfold (→ payments, advisor-confirmed),
cashbox (→ POS/ATM, advisor-confirmed), monthbar (→ calendar app), jar v2
(open-mouth rebuild, archived in batch2), and two survivors:

- **Piggy (solid silhouette)** — advisor: *highest* private-budget fit,
  best mass read; the solid body finally survives 32 px (outline pigs kept
  reading as aliens). Coin above = amber element on Pro, top of glyph.
- **Hearth (house + coin PAIR)** — owner caught the single-ring coin reading as a bullseye; rebuilt as two overlapping coins (advisor: reads money, zero target/eye risk; mailbox-floor variant rejected same pass). Advisor's strongest *pairing* with
  Vault; the deepest story (money that stays inside the house = offline).
  BUT side-by-side shelf test (finalists.png) shows the two houses read
  nearly twin in the Free colorway — "Vault" vs "Vault's second app"
  confusion risk for the owner's own two-product shelf.

Failure pattern found across ~18 glyphs: narrow-neck vessels → bottle/
inkwell; complex organic outlines → mush; coin+line → strikethrough;
open book → guidebook. What works in this house system: **simple vessel or
solid animal + one disc accent.**

**Agent recommendation: Piggy.** Mass instantly reads budget/savings
(the owner's core complaint, solved), silhouette is unmistakably distinct
from Vault's house while sharing its grammar (container + kept thing +
one top accent), and it survives every launcher mask at every size.
Hearth stays the documented brand-family alternative if the twin-house
trade-off is accepted.

Advisory (ollamatest, verified): best_for_private_budget = pigsolid;
category risks noted: pig reads savings-adjacent (savings/investing apps)
— acceptable, same shelf neighborhood as budgeting.
